Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

PlexBook does not collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers. All playback positions and preferences are stored locally in your browser or device.

When you connect to a Plex server, authentication is handled directly between your browser and Plex.tv. PlexBook does not store your Plex credentials on any external server.

No analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising SDKs are used.
