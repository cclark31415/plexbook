About PlexBook

PlexBook is a free audiobook player that streams non-DRM audiobooks from your Plex media server. You can also add local audio files directly. Multiple sources are merged into a single library sorted alphabetically.

Features

  • Stream audiobooks from any Plex server via JWT authentication
  • Play local audio files or entire folders as books
  • Mixed library from multiple sources, sorted alphabetically
  • Chapter selection and playback speed from 0.25x to 3x
  • Automatic position saving every 10 seconds
  • Album art display with optional audio spectrum visualizer
  • Light and dark themes
  • Works as a web app and Android app
